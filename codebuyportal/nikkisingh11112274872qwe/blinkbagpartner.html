01. Market Financials & Growth

The UK Digital Out-of-Home Surge

The UK OOH advertising market achieved an unprecedented £1.44 billion valuation in 2025, driven heavily by rapid digitization. In Q2 2026 alone, OOH revenues jumped 12.7% YoY to £402.6 million, with Digital Out-of-Home (DOOH) capturing 68% of total market revenue. Advertisers are increasingly shifting spend to dynamic, programmatic platforms (pDOOH).

UK OOH Total Revenue vs. DOOH Revenue Share (2022–2026)

Source: Outsmart / PwC

DOOH revenue has grown from 63% in 2022 to 68% in 2026, demonstrating an irreversible structural shift toward digital screen media.

📈

Programmatic Acceleration

77% of UK media planners now buy OOH programmatically. Industry leaders like JCDecaux experienced a 30.9% organic growth in programmatic revenue in H1 2026 via SSPs like VIOOH and platforms like The Trade Desk.

Universal Weekly Saturation

Outdoor advertising reaches 98% of the UK population weekly. Billboards deliver irreplaceable scale and prestige across major arterial highways and city centers.
